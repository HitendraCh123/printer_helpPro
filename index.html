How to Proceed with Printer Setup?

  1. 1Unbox your printer and connect it to a stable power source.
  2. 2Install the ink or toner cartridges as shown in the quick-start guide.
  3. 3Load compatible paper into the input tray and adjust the paper guides to fit.
  4. 4Set your preferred language, region, and date/time from the control panel.
  5. 5Download and install the latest official driver for your operating system.
  6. 6Print a test page to confirm the connection and check print quality.
FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

Are printer drivers actually free to download?

Yes, always. Genuine printer drivers are free directly from the manufacturer. We link straight to those official pages, so you never need to pay for a driver download.

Why does my printer show “offline” even though it's connected?

It's almost always a software or network-settings issue, not a hardware fault. Start with the offline checklist below, or visit our Diagnose page for a full walkthrough.

Do I need to reinstall the driver after a Windows or macOS update?

Sometimes. Major OS updates occasionally reset print settings or break driver compatibility. If your printer stops responding right after an update, reinstalling the latest driver usually fixes it.

Can I set up a printer without the installation CD?

Yes — most current printers no longer need one. Download the latest driver from the manufacturer's official site instead; it will always be more current than the disc that shipped in the box.

Common Issues

How to Fix a Printer Offline Issue

If your printer has abruptly stopped responding, "offline" status is the first thing to troubleshoot. Here are the fastest ways to get it back online:

01

Check every cable connection — make sure the USB or network cable is fully seated.

02

Manually set the printer to “online” from your operating system's printer settings.

03

Clear any stuck jobs from the print queue before sending a new one.

04

Reinstall the driver — an outdated or corrupted driver is the most common cause of offline errors.

05

Run the manufacturer's built-in print-and-scan diagnostic tool, if available.

06

Open the printer and check carefully for a hidden paper jam.
